## Color Scheme (Reference)

- **Primary**: Blue (#3B82F6) - Actions, links, active states
- **Success**: Green (#10B981) - Paid status, positive metrics
- **Warning**: Amber (#F59E0B) - Overdue, attention needed
- **Danger**: Red (#EF4444) - Void, delete actions
- **Neutral**: Gray scale for text and backgrounds

## Mobile Considerations

- Navigation collapses to hamburger menu
- Quick entry form stacks vertically
- Entry cards become full-width
- Filters collapse to expandable section
- Actions move to bottom of cards

---

## Component Patterns

### Cards
All cards use consistent padding, rounded corners (8px), and subtle shadow.

### Tables
Tables convert to card layout on mobile for better readability.

### Forms
Labels above inputs, consistent spacing, clear error states.

### Status Badges
- Draft: Gray outline
- Sent: Blue filled
- Paid: Green filled
- Void: Red outline
- Overdue: Amber filled with warning icon
